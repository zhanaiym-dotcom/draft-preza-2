What does the literature suggest?

E1 · Social sanctions

Expected to be the most frequently documented form.

Lewis & Christin (2022); Arystanbek (2023); Meyer (2024).

E2 · Institutional sanctions

Expected to occur less frequently than informal social sanctions.

Bartz et al. (2013).

E3 · Legal consequences

Expected to be less frequently documented than informal social sanctions.

Akisheva (2025); Kainazarova et al. (2025); Hörnle (2021).

Descriptive expectations, not causal hypotheses or predetermined findings.

05 / Conceptualization

Defining the key concepts

Accountability

A relationship between an actor and a forum in which the actor may justify their conduct, the forum can question and judge it, and consequences may follow.

Bovens (2007)

Public figures

Individuals who held a publicly recognized role or status in Kazakhstan before the accusation.

Cover et al. (2025)

Gender-based violence

Violence directed at individuals because of their gender or arising from gender inequalities.

United Nations Police (n.d.)

08 / Measurement quality

Validity and reliability

Validity

  • Indicators correspond to theoretical categories.
  • Observable sanctions provide concrete evidence.
  • Indicators capture outcomes rather than the entire process.
  • Undocumented sanctions may reduce accuracy.

Reliability

  • Standardized coding rules.
  • Cross-checking independent sources.
  • Consistent selection criteria.
  • Distinction between missing information and zero.
  • Planned pilot coding.
Public visibility and the gender-related nature of violence may require contextual judgment.

PLS 210 / RESEARCH PROPOSAL II10 — 16
10 / Case selection

Criterion-based purposive sampling

25   Target number of cases

Inclusion criteria

  • Publicly recognized before the accusation.
  • Accusation concerns GBV.
  • First reported November 2023–September 2026.
  • Connected to Kazakhstan.

Exclusion criteria

  • Visibility gained only after accusation.
  • Social-media-only allegations.
  • First reported outside the period.
  • No identifiable connection to gender.
Include cases without documented consequences to avoid selection based on the outcome.

PLS 210 / RESEARCH PROPOSAL II13 — 16
13 / Research integrity

Ethics and limitations

Ethical considerations

  • Public secondary data only.
  • No direct participant interaction.
  • Distinguish accusations from established guilt.
  • Protect sensitive personal information.
  • Password-protected storage.
  • No identifiable case data entered into AI tools.

Limitations and mitigation

LimitationMitigation
Media coverage and selection biasSystematic bilingual searches
Incomplete evidence and measurementCross-verification and separate NA coding
Unequal observation periodsRecord dates and observation periods
The study cannot establish causality or estimate actual prevalence across Kazakhstan.
